# Deck style rules

Applies to UX audit decks, proposal slide decks and Upwork case-study slides.
These are the rules from feedback on the Miss Money Savvy audit. Colours, fonts and
overall mood are **not decided yet**: they come from the moodboard, and once approved
they become Figma variables. Until then, use a neutral light theme.

## Canvas

- Slides are 1920 × 1080. Upwork case-study images are 1000 × 750 (see the
  "Figma Case Study Design Guide" page in Notion).
- Content uses the full slide; no half-empty layouts with a small block in the middle.

## Type

- Minimum sizes on 1920 × 1080: body 28 px, captions and table text 22 px,
  headlines 56–72 px.
- Headlines are plain and descriptive. No clever or punny phrasing.
- **No eyebrow labels** (small uppercase text above headlines, e.g. "002 · THE ROUTE IN").
  The area name sits once in the slide header, in the same position on every slide.
- No numbered section codes (001, 002…).

## Layout

- **No side strokes or accent bars** on text blocks. Separate with spacing, or a subtle
  background only when grouping truly needs it.
- **No section-divider slides.** Areas are introduced by their first finding slide.
- Screenshots take at least 55% of the slide on finding slides.

## Images

- Shown whole, or cropped deliberately to the exact problem region. Never squashed,
  never cropped to a thin strip that hides the point of the slide.
- One consistent frame style for all screenshots (to be set with the moodboard);
  no heavy drop shadows.
- Numbered markers on the image match the numbered text next to it.
- Laptop and phone mockups: place the screenshot as an image fill in a mockup frame
  in Figma.
